## Scope and completeness boundary

UX Pilot describes its blog as material on user experience, interface and product design. The live archive currently exposes five pages, but public search/cache snapshots are inconsistent: the current first page reports `Page 1 of 5`, while an older cached page reports `Page 5 of 14`. Pages beyond the current live boundary return empty archives.

This inventory therefore records every design article discoverable through the current live archive, official indexed article pages and the older public cache available on the access date. It is comprehensive for the discoverable public snapshot, but it does not claim that deleted, unindexed or dynamically hidden historical articles were read.

Tool reviews, comparisons and trend roundups are inventoried for provenance. They are not automatically promoted to MoneyFlow design requirements.
