## The three territories

Each derives from a different trust mechanism in first-party financial software
documentation rather than from a mood, which is how they end up genuinely different:

- **A — Trang Sổ (the ruled register).** The collection is the hero; traceability is
  spatial, from Oracle GL and Dynamics balance-to-journal drill-down. Densest, closest to
  what ships, weakest on recovery expression.
- **B — Biên Nhận (the attested record).** The object is the hero; provenance is printed
  on the row, from Business Central's audit-trail codes. Strongest on mobile and on manual
  capture; lowest list density.
- **C — Bản Ghi & Bản Sửa (state and amendment).** The timeline is the hero; from
  plain-text accounting's history-as-states, correction becomes the signature behaviour.
  Most distinctive, most expensive, depends on capability that does not exist.

Two reference refusals are load-bearing: Business Central's **immutability** is not copied
because MoneyFlow deliberately allows edit with soft delete and undo, and plain-text
accounting's file/CLI interface is not copied because the user is not operating a tool.

## What the evaluation caught

The most useful findings were structural rather than aesthetic:

- **Each territory had 16 elements, not 17.** The missing one was **element 1, the
  territory name**, which I had put only in the heading — so every element after 10 was
  mis-numbered and seven cross-references pointed at the wrong section.
- **The money-without-colour table overstated its own channels.** A's "column position"
  cannot separate income from expense when A has one amount column; B counted one channel
  twice; C's structural channels are spent on *state*, so kind reduced to sign plus glyph,
  and transfers, being signless, resolved on glyph alone. Balance and transfer are both
  signless, so absence of a sign never separated them. Each territory now has a real
  three-channel kind system and balance is separated structurally.
- **Territory A did not express recovery at all**, which Phase D requires of all three,
  and its stated correction model — "a new line in the register" — was the
  correction-by-new-entry pattern its own reference ledger forbids.
- **Territory C proposed amber for amendment**, colliding with the reserved warning hue
  and contradicting its own rule that change is shown as record, never as alert. State now
  uses no new hue at all, which also removes the accessibility trap.
- **A annexed B's differentiator** by carrying a per-row origin column; iconography and
  graphic language were one answer written three times.
- **"Sổ Cái" is the Vietnamese term for *general ledger*** — the accounting terminology my
  own reference ledger says not to import, and which Phase D's A8 bans. Renamed.
- Six places in the Trust parent still said Phase E was unopened, and it carried a stale
  baseline.
