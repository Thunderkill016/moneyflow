# PR #431 — docs(product): replace the platform direction with a survival direction

- Date: 2026-08-18
- Change class: Class 0 — documentation only. No code, schema, RLS, Auth, provider, CI or financial-semantics change. It does, however, change the highest product authority in the repository, so it needs an owner decision rather than a review.
- Affected capability or project boundary: `docs/product/MONEYFLOW_PRODUCT_VISION.md`, which is marked "binding long-term product direction" and dated `Owner decision: 2026-08-03`. Also narrows the horizon framing in `PRINCIPLES.md` and marks `TARGET_ARCHITECTURE_ROADMAP.md` and `GLOBAL_PERSONAL_FINANCE_CAPABILITY_ATLAS.md` as superseded/reference.
- Status impact: candidate until merged. On merge it becomes the product authority and `CURRENT_PROJECT_MEMORY.md` should record the direction change, because product direction is durable truth.
- Why: the previous direction committed the product to becoming a "comprehensive personal-finance platform" across six pillars and eight waves. It is a plan about capability; the product's actual risk is survival — one owner, zero users, unlaunched, four P1 gates blocked on owner/lawyer. The evidence that the gap is real is in this repository's merged history: #406 superseded by #408, #410 by #412, #412's presentation by #414. Three of five releases replaced by the next one, which is what an infinite roadmap produces against finite capacity.
- Changed: the direction is now three testable constraints — zero marginal cost per user, payoff before capture speed, a fixed surface. "Approved horizons" become explicit rule-outs, each with a falsifiable condition that would reopen it. Personas "power user" and "household/adviser" removed, because listing a persona creates roadmap pressure toward it. Levels 4–6 removed. The six-pillar system withdrawn. Success measures narrowed to what can actually be read at zero users. A new product law 11 (no recurring per-user cost without revenue) is the one that decides the hard cases.
- New §10 on bank connection, researched 2026-08-18: Circular 64/2024/TT-NHNN (effective 2025-03-01, full compliance 2027-03-01) restricts payment-initiation and e-wallet Open APIs to third parties that are banks or licensed intermediary payment service providers. Whether read-only account data carries the same condition is **not** established here — that needs the Circular text, not press summaries, and the document says so. The practical route without a licence is an aggregator, which reintroduces the per-user data cost that closed Mint (~25M accounts, shut down January 2024). So bank sync is ruled out by the cost constraint, not by regulation.
- Recorded as the cheaper substitute, with its current defect stated rather than hidden: the app already registers a `share_target` and parses shared notifications through `src/lib/inbox/parse-text.ts`. Measured against 8 real-format Vietnamese bank SMS it parses 8/8, classifies 7/8 correctly and extracts **4/8 amounts wrong** — every failure taking the account number as the amount. That measurement is from this session and is not yet covered by a regression test; the fix is not in this PR.
- Downstream documents were marked, not deleted. The architecture roadmap keeps its seam analysis and its refusal to create microservices without evidence; the capability atlas stays as research into how other products solved problems MoneyFlow already has. Deleting them would have destroyed usable research to make a point about scope.
- Verified: `check:knowledge` (passes, with the pre-existing `CURRENT_PROJECT_MEMORY.md` compaction warning), `test:ci-policy`, `git diff --check`, `npm test` (1093 pass). No browser, database or provider gate applies to a documentation change.
- Production/provider evidence: not applicable. No provider write, deployment or production-data access.
- Remaining: the parser amount-extraction defect recorded above is measured but unfixed, and has no regression test — that is the next product work under the new §1.2, not part of this PR. Vision §10 says it is not established whether read-only account-data Open APIs carry the same licensing condition as payment initiation; answering that needs the Circular text and is left open rather than guessed. `CURRENT_PROJECT_MEMORY.md` is over its compaction threshold (229 lines) and was left alone here, because compacting it inside a direction change would mix two edits with different review needs.
- Snapshot update: not applicable while Status impact remains candidate. On merge, `CURRENT_PROJECT_MEMORY.md` should record the direction change in a follow-up, because product direction is durable truth and an unmerged direction is candidate evidence.

Does not supersede #403, #417, #418, #426, any RRB gate, or `docs/MVP_DEFINITION.md`, which still records what the 2026-08-03 release required.
