- Late measurement findings, recorded rather than smoothed over. First, the LCP noise floor survives medianing: the `/` LCP **median** drifts 2808 → 2952 → 3100 ms across three runs of behaviourally identical code (the heads differ only by an `aria-busy` attribute, comments and docs), a 292 ms spread in the medians on top of 156–460 ms within-run ranges. That is several times the −99 ms once read as a `/dashboard` gain, so three samples are not enough for this task's question. Second, `/dashboard` FCP is **bimodal** at ~1077 ms versus ~1690 ms with nothing in between, so medians over mixed samples report a mode rather than a centre; the low mode appeared 2 of 3 times on `4ea4746` and 1 of 3 on `bba61f9`, and 0 of 4 earlier. It reproduces across two independent CI runs and is route-specific — `/` has no loading boundary and never shows a low mode, staying at 1675–1710 ms throughout — which makes an earlier first paint of roughly 615 ms when the boundary paints the one genuinely promising signal here. It remains an open question, not a claim: the no-boundary control is one CI sample plus one local median run, and the mechanism is inferred from timing rather than a recorded paint element. The experiment it needs is many samples per side with and without the boundary on one runner, reported as a mode split.
