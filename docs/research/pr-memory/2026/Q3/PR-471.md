# PR #471 — fix(planning): stop reporting unpaid bills as free money

- Date: 2026-08-26
- Change class: Class 2 — corrects the financial semantics of one derived figure on the planning surface, with two narrowed server reads. No schema, migration, RLS, RPC or provider change; no ledger mutation.
- Affected capability or project boundary: the `/budgets` unassigned figure, `src/lib/planning/allocation.ts` and `getBudgetsWorkspace`.

- Affected capability or project boundary detail: the figure is the first brick of a budgeting method, so a figure that reads high is worse than no figure — it invites exactly the over-commitment the method exists to prevent.
- The defect, and it was mine: #469 computed `unassigned = income − budget limits`. `RecurringCommitment` carries `amount`, `dueDay`, `categoryId` and `isPaid`, and an unpaid commitment is a real claim on this month's income that no budget necessarily covers. With income 20,000,000 ₫, limits 8,000,000 ₫ and an unpaid 6,000,000 ₫ bill, the page reported 12,000,000 ₫ unassigned when 6,000,000 ₫ was free — a 100% overstatement, in the harmful direction.
- Why the obvious fix would have been wrong: adding every commitment amount to the allocated total double-counts. A rent commitment of 5,000,000 ₫ in a category whose budget is already 5,000,000 ₫ is allocated once, by that limit. The rule shipped is therefore `allocated = budget limits + unpaid commitments whose category has no budget for this month`, so each đồng is claimed exactly once.
- Changed: new `uncoveredCommitmentTotal` applying that coverage rule; `MonthAllocation` now reports `limits` and `committed` separately alongside their sum, because folding bills into one "allocated" total would hide the half the reader did not choose; `allocateMonthFromTotals` takes `limits` plus optional `committed`; `allocateMonth` accepts month-resolved commitments; `allocationExplanation` names bills apart from limits, and keeps its previous wording when there are none rather than printing "+ 0". `getBudgetsWorkspace` gains reads of `recurring_commitment_feed` and `commitment_occurrences` filtered to the selected month, reusing the exported `mapCommitmentRow` rather than re-deriving due-date and paid state.
- A second freshness defect of the same kind was found and fixed before it shipped: the workspace first returned a precomputed `monthCommitted` total. That would have kept counting a bill after the user gave its category a budget, until the next page load. The workspace now returns the resolved commitment list and the page applies coverage against live client budget state, so the figure corrects itself the moment a budget is added, edited or removed.
- Cases pinned by test: an unpaid bill in an unbudgeted category reduces unassigned by its amount; a bill whose category has a budget contributes zero, asserted with a message naming the double-count; a paid bill contributes zero because it is already a recorded expense; archived bills and occurrences due in another month contribute zero; bills can move a month from `unallocated` to `over`; the explanation names both bill and limit operands when bills exist and omits the clause when they do not.
- The advice line is still guarded: the vocabulary test now also runs the bill-bearing wording across four states, failing on `nên|đừng|hãy|cảnh báo|vượt mức|tiết kiệm|cắt giảm|quá nhiều|hạn chế|khuyên|cẩn thận|coi chừng|còn có thể tiêu`.
