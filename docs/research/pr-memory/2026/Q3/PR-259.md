## Research evidence

Official current product/help documentation was reviewed for:

- Actual Budget: reconciliation, schedules and ordered rules;
- YNAB: targets and debt/loan planning;
- Monarch Money: flex/category budgets and collaboration;
- PocketSmith: forecasts, scenarios and multi-currency;
- Lunch Money: extensibility, API and multi-currency;
- Copilot Money: transaction review, recurring attention and alerts;
- Tiller: user-controlled transaction feeds and templates;
- Firefly III: deterministic rule behavior and AGPL reuse boundary.

The documents record principles and limits; no competitor code, private data or visual identity is copied.

## Boundaries

- No runtime, database, provider, deployment or production-data behavior changes.
- No future capability is current product behavior until merged implementation evidence exists.
- Bank sync, AI, household, investments, multi-currency and native applications remain separately gated.
- Integer VND, transfer neutrality, RLS, recoverability and no-guessed-finance laws remain binding.
- The current modular monolith remains the chosen architecture; the roadmap does not authorize microservices or a rewrite.
