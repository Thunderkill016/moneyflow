## What the strategy actually decides

One mechanism: every **ledger-derived** financial figure should trace back to the
records behind it, and every ordinary mistake in those records should be correctable.
Records exist because the user entered them or supplied and accepted the data they came
from — manual capture is the default identity, direct CSV import is secondary. The Vietnamese category sells the opposite
trade — first-party listings promise "liên kết ngân hàng, mọi giao dịch tự cập nhật"
and "Biết tiền đi đâu — không cần tự nhớ", backed by institutional scale. MoneyFlow has
no bank connection, no classifier and no institution, so its trust cannot be borrowed;
it must be demonstrated per record. Manual entry is therefore a differentiator only
through provenance, and is recorded as an anti-differentiator when sold as effort.

## The evaluation's most useful catches

Two claims at the centre of the document were false against shipped code:

- **Aggregate drill-down does not exist.** Report category rows are not links and both
  Reports and Overview link to an unscoped `/transactions`, even though the route
  accepts scope params. Phase C had already classified this as ADAPT LATER. The
  document now states the mechanism as half-shipped — correctable yes, traceable not
  yet — rather than as a current property.
- **"Every row was typed by the user" is false.** Direct CSV import writes approved
  rows without passing the Inbox review queue.

Both were corrected in place rather than softened, because a brand document that
overstates the product is the specific failure this phase exists to prevent.
