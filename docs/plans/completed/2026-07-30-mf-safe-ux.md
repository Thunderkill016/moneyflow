## Confirmed root causes

1. Mobile landing CSS intentionally hid the Login action at phone widths.
2. Authenticated Dashboard and Inbox used different data authorities for pending counts.
3. Legacy route styles fragmented responsive and amount-surface behavior.
4. The phone transaction day header remained sticky and overlaid the first row.
5. Signed-in route transitions could retain document scroll beneath the sticky header.
6. Mobile workspace and sheets did not consistently reserve navigation and safe-area clearance.
7. Account surfaces and action styles applied semantic color where neutral presentation was required.
8. The 23 `SECURITY DEFINER` RPCs were intentional authenticated browser APIs, but required explicit grant, identity, ownership, `search_path` and tenant-isolation evidence.

## Security contract retained

- `PUBLIC` and `anon` cannot execute privileged mutation RPCs.
- Intentional browser RPCs are limited to `authenticated`.
- Privileged mutations derive identity from `auth.uid()`, reject unauthenticated execution, check ownership and lock `search_path`.
- Financial invariants and cross-tenant isolation remain covered by fresh reset and pgTAP.
- Leaked-password protection is not represented as fixed while unavailable under the current Supabase plan.

## UI contract retained

- Mobile landing exposes a clear Login action with a practical touch target.
- Authenticated Inbox counts use server/RLS state; demo counts remain device-local.
- Shared tokens own neutral and semantic amount surfaces.
- Phone transaction day totals remain above rows without overlap.
- Signed-in content begins below the app header and clears bottom navigation/safe areas.
- Sheets retain reachable final controls and contained overscroll.
- Neutral account totals do not use positive/green semantic fills.
- The central mobile capture action remains canonical transaction capture.
- Dark amount inputs and account actions maintain readable contrast.
